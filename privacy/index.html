Privacy Policy

Arrow Maze: Puzzle Escape · Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Controller: Vaibhav Pandya (contact below)

The short version

Arrow Maze: Puzzle Escape stores all gameplay progress on your device only. We don't run our own servers, don't require an account, and don't use third-party analytics SDKs. The only third parties that touch your device are Apple (purchases) and Google (advertising).

What is stored on your device

  • Your level progress, star ratings, hint inventory, streaks and settings — stored via iOS UserDefaults, synced only by your device's own iCloud backup. We cannot read it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The app shows a banner on the menu, an occasional interstitial between levels, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (extra hints / extra hearts). Removing ads permanently (one-time purchase) removes banner and interstitial ads; watching rewarded ads always remains your choice.
  • Consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): before any ad is requested, a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form is shown where required. If you decline, or no consent is given, the app requests non-personalized ads only (or no ads). You can revisit your choice through your device's tracking settings.
  • ATT (iOS): after your first level win — never on a cold launch — iOS asks whether the app may use the device's advertising identifier for personalized ads. Declining limits Google to non-personalized delivery.
  • What Google may collect: device identifiers (IDFA/advertising ID), IP-derived coarse location, ad interactions, coarse device/app info, and measurement data via SKAdNetwork. Google is the controller for that data under Google's Privacy Policy; see also AdMob data disclosure.

Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only proof of purchase via StoreKit to flag your account as ad-free on-device.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (daily challenge, streak protection) are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server; denial of permission removes all reminders with no impact on gameplay.

Children

The app is a puzzle game, not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. Advertising content may be filtered by your device's Screen Time settings.

Your rights

Because everything stays on your device, you can delete all progress at any time via Settings → Reset All Progress (Remove Ads ownership is kept), or by deleting the app.

Contact
